Professional Profile

Dedicated to Understanding Rheumatological Conditions

Dr. Muhammad Qousain Ali is a Rheumatology Specialist with postgraduate training in rheumatology.

His professional qualifications include MBBS, BSC, an EULAR Postgraduate Certificate of Rheumatology from Europe, and a Postgraduate Diploma in Rheumatology from Wales, UK.

Rheumatology involves the evaluation and management of conditions affecting joints, muscles, bones and the immune system. Patients may experience symptoms such as joint pain, stiffness, swelling or other musculoskeletal problems.

The goal of a specialist consultation is to understand the patient's symptoms, medical history and clinical needs so that appropriate care can be discussed.
