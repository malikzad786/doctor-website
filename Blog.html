Rheumatology Guide

What Does Rheumatology Cover?

Rheumatology is a medical specialty concerned with conditions involving joints, muscles, bones and the immune system.

People may seek medical advice for symptoms such as persistent joint pain, swelling, stiffness, difficulty with movement or recurring musculoskeletal symptoms.

Different conditions can have similar symptoms, so the cause of symptoms cannot always be determined from symptoms alone. A qualified healthcare professional can evaluate the individual clinical situation.

Medical Disclaimer

The information on this blog is intended for general educational purposes only. It does not replace a consultation, diagnosis or treatment plan from a qualified healthcare professional.

If you have persistent, severe or concerning symptoms, seek appropriate medical advice.
