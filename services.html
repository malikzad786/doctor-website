Understanding Rheumatological Conditions

Rheumatology covers many conditions that can affect the joints, muscles, bones and immune system. The following information provides a general introduction to common areas of care.

Rheumatoid Arthritisریمیٹائیڈ آرتھرائٹس

Rheumatoid arthritis is an inflammatory autoimmune condition that commonly affects joints. Symptoms may include pain, swelling and stiffness.

Osteoarthritisاوسٹیو آرتھرائٹس

Osteoarthritis is a common joint condition that can cause pain, stiffness and reduced movement.

Systemic lupus erythematosus is an autoimmune condition that can affect different parts of the body.

When to Seek Advice

Persistent Joint or Musculoskeletal Symptoms?

Persistent or recurring symptoms such as joint pain, swelling, stiffness or other musculoskeletal concerns may require medical evaluation.

A rheumatology consultation can provide an opportunity to discuss symptoms, medical history and appropriate next steps.

Common Symptoms

  • Persistent joint pain
  • Joint swelling
  • Morning stiffness
  • Difficulty moving a joint
  • Recurring inflammatory symptoms
  • Unexplained musculoskeletal symptoms
